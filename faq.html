Questions

Short answers. Anything else, ask us.

What is Whiff?

Whiff is a debt payoff app. Every time money lands in your account, Whiff tells you a safe amount to put toward one debt, and the rest stays yours.

What counts as money coming in?

Any real deposit. A paycheck, a side gig payout, a friend paying you back, something you sold, birthday money, a refund. Moving money between your own accounts and interest don't count.

How does Whiff decide how much to put toward my debt?

It looks at what came in and what your bills and life need, then suggests one amount that's safe to put toward your debt. It's an estimate, and you always decide.

Why does Whiff split my debt into pieces?

One big number is easy to avoid. Whiff splits a debt into at most six pieces, and each piece fills with five stamps. Finishing a piece feels like a win, and research on debt payoff shows those small wins help people keep going.

Does Whiff tell me where I'm wasting money?

It can point out one habit to trim a little. For example, 20 coffees a month could be 15, and the other 5 go toward your debt. No budget to keep, and no guilt.

What is Whiff Moments?

Moments shows real Whiff members paying off real debt. Signed-in people can give toward a member's debt after seeing their effort: when they joined, where they started, where they are now, and every payment.

Do I have to ask for help in Moments?

No, and you can't. There are no posts asking for money. Members keep paying, and their progress does the talking. Joining Moments is optional.

Where does a Moments gift go?

Toward the member's debt. It can't be cashed out. Gifts aren't charity and aren't tax-deductible.

What do other people see about me in Moments?

Your first name (or a name you pick) and your progress. Never your lender, account numbers, email, or phone number.

Is my bank information safe?

Accounts connect through trusted third-party partners, and Whiff never sees your bank or lender passwords. Gifts need a signed-in account and have daily limits to stop card fraud. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

Is Whiff a lender or a debt-relief company?

No. Whiff isn't a bank, lender, debt-relief company, or financial advisor, and it doesn't negotiate with your creditors. You keep paying your lenders as usual. Whiff helps you pay more, more often.

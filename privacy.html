Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

This policy explains how Whiff, Inc. ("Whiff," "we," "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Whiff app, Whiff Moments, and this website (the "Service"). The short version: we use your information to run Whiff, we don't sell it, and in Moments other people see your first name and progress, never your lender or accounts.

1. What we collect

You give us: your name or nickname, email, password, mobile number, and, when you choose to find your debts, your date of birth. Your date of birth is used once to match you at the credit bureau and isn't kept by Whiff. Also anything you send us, like messages through our contact form, and notes you write in Moments.

From accounts you connect (through our partners): account and balance details, debts (such as balances, rates, and lenders), deposits into your accounts, and spending details like category, amount, and date. We use deposits to know when money comes in, and limited spending details to suggest one habit you could trim. We keep spending details for about 60 days.

Automatically: basic device and usage information, such as app version, device type, IP address, and crash logs, so we can keep Whiff secure and working.

On this website: we don't use advertising or analytics cookies. Our host keeps standard server logs (like IP address and pages requested) for security.

2. How we use it

  • To run Whiff: find your debts, notice money coming in, and suggest amounts toward your debt.
  • To run Moments: show your first name and progress to signed-in users if you join, and process gifts.
  • To keep people safe: verify identity, prevent fraud and abuse, enforce limits, and handle reports and blocks.
  • To talk with you: answer messages and send important account and service notices.
  • To improve Whiff, using information in a way that doesn't identify you where we can.
  • To meet legal obligations.

3. What others see in Moments

Only if you join Moments, signed-in users can see your first name (or the name you choose), when you joined, your starting and current balance, and your payment history. Helpers' names and notes are shown to the person they helped. We never show your lender, account numbers, email, or phone number. You can leave Moments any time in Profile.

4. Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising. We share it only:

  • With service providers who help us run Whiff, such as account-connection and credit-data providers, payment processors, hosting, email, and customer support, under contracts that limit their use of it.
  • With other users, as described in section 3.
  • If the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of Whiff, our users, or others, including to prevent fraud.
  • In a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case this policy continues to apply.
  • With your permission.

Our account-connection partners handle your bank login. Whiff never sees or stores your bank or lender passwords.

5. How long we keep it

We keep information while your account is open and as long as needed to provide the Service, then delete or de-identify it, unless we must keep it longer for legal, tax, fraud-prevention, or dispute reasons.

6. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls, and other safeguards. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a unique password and tell us if you notice anything unusual.

7. Your choices and rights

  • See, correct, or delete your information, or download a copy.
  • Disconnect accounts, leave Moments, or close your Whiff account.
  • Opt out of non-essential emails using the link in them.

Depending on where you live (for example, California), you may have extra rights, including to know what we collect and to not be discriminated against for using your rights. To make a request, contact us. We'll verify your identity before acting, and you can use an authorized agent.

8. Children

Whiff is for adults 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

9. Where we operate

Whiff is offered in the United States, and information is stored and processed in the United States.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
